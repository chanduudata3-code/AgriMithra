Rice

Oryza sativa

Rice is one of the world's major food crops. It grows well in warm and humid conditions and generally requires a reliable supply of water during its main growth stages.

🌡️ Temperature 20°C – 35°C
🌧️ Rainfall 100–200 cm
🪴 Suitable Soil Clay / Clay Loam
📅 Duration 100–150 days

🌱 Growing Conditions

Rice prefers warm weather, good water availability and soils capable of retaining moisture.

💧 Irrigation

Water management is especially important during establishment, tillering, flowering and grain development.

☀️ Climate

Warm and humid conditions generally support good vegetative growth and grain formation.

🔬 Disease Detection

AgriMitra can use a rice leaf image to identify supported rice disease classes.

CROP 02

Cotton

Gossypium

Cotton is an important fibre crop that performs well under warm conditions. Good drainage, adequate sunlight and careful water management are important for healthy crop development.

🌡️ Temperature 21°C – 30°C
🌧️ Rainfall 50–100 cm
🪴 Suitable Soil Black / Loamy Soil
📅 Duration 150–180 days

🌱 Growing Conditions

Cotton grows best in warm conditions with sufficient sunlight and well-drained soil.

💧 Irrigation

Irrigation requirements depend on rainfall, soil and growth stage. Waterlogging should generally be avoided.

☀️ Climate

A warm growing period and relatively dry conditions during boll opening are generally favorable.

CROP 03

Maize

Zea mays

Maize is a widely cultivated cereal crop used for food, animal feed and industrial products. It grows well in warm weather with adequate sunlight and fertile, well-drained soil.

🌡️ Temperature 18°C – 27°C
🌧️ Rainfall 50–100 cm
🪴 Suitable Soil Loamy Soil
📅 Duration 90–120 days

🌱 Growing Conditions

Maize prefers fertile, well-drained soil, sufficient sunlight and moderate moisture during its growth period.

💧 Irrigation

Water availability is particularly important around flowering, silking and grain-filling stages.

☀️ Climate

Warm temperatures with good sunlight generally support strong plant growth and grain development.

🔬 Disease Detection

The maize model can later classify supported leaf conditions such as rust and leaf blight.

CROP 04

Jowar

Sorghum bicolor

Jowar, or sorghum, is an important cereal crop known for its ability to tolerate relatively dry conditions. It is cultivated for grain, fodder and several other agricultural uses.

🌡️ Temperature 25°C – 32°C
🌧️ Rainfall 40–100 cm
🪴 Suitable Soil Loamy / Black Soil
📅 Duration 100–130 days

🌱 Growing Conditions

Jowar performs well in warm conditions and can tolerate lower rainfall better than many water-intensive crops.

💧 Irrigation

Irrigation may be required during prolonged dry periods, especially at important crop development stages.

☀️ Climate

Warm weather and good sunlight are favorable, while excessive standing water should be avoided.

🔬 Disease Detection

Jowar images can later be processed using the trained sorghum disease classification model.
