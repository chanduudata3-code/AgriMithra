SOIL GUIDE

Understand Your Soil

Learn about common agricultural soil types, their basic properties and suitable crops.

Soil plays a major role in crop growth

Different crops perform better under different soil conditions. Explore the common soil types below and understand their basic characteristics.

🟤
SOIL TYPE 01

Alluvial Soil

Alluvial soil is commonly found in river plains and is generally fertile. Its texture can range from sandy loam to clay depending on the location.

Texture Sandy Loam – Clay
Fertility Generally High
Drainage Moderate
Water Holding Moderate – High

Suitable AgriMitra Crops

🌾 Rice 🌽 Maize
⚫
SOIL TYPE 02

Black Soil

Black soil is known for its high clay content and moisture-retaining ability. It is strongly associated with cotton cultivation in India.

Texture Clayey
Fertility Moderate – High
Drainage Slow
Water Holding High

Suitable AgriMitra Crops

🌿 Cotton 🌾 Jowar
🟠
SOIL TYPE 03

Red Soil

Red soil gets its characteristic colour largely from iron compounds. Its fertility and texture vary considerably across regions.

Texture Sandy – Loamy
Fertility Low – Moderate
Drainage Good
Water Holding Low – Moderate

Suitable AgriMitra Crops

🌽 Maize 🌾 Jowar 🌿 Cotton
🟫
SOIL TYPE 04

Laterite Soil

Laterite soils develop under conditions of high temperature and rainfall. Heavy leaching can reduce their natural nutrient content.

Texture Gravelly – Loamy
Fertility Generally Low
Drainage Good
Water Holding Low – Moderate

Suitability

Depends on management
💡

Soil type alone is not enough

Crop suitability also depends on soil pH, nutrients, drainage, climate, rainfall, irrigation and local conditions. A soil test provides more reliable information before making fertilizer or crop-management decisions.
